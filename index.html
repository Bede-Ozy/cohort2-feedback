Question 21 · 25 meaningful responses

What would you say are the programme’s greatest strengths, and what are its most important areas for improvement?

Strengths to preserve

People, projects and exposure

Written feedback repeatedly highlights committed coaches, practical project work, professional development, guest leaders and mentorship.

Learning design

More time to practise

Students want more hands-on time between tools, space to revise and practise independently, and deadlines that are easier to balance.

Support priorities

Build skills and confidence

Additional SQL and Power BI practice, clearer technical support, and more chances to present insights are recurring improvement themes.

Learning environment

Make practice more accessible

Some feedback calls for improved access to a suitable place, equipment, connectivity and time to continue learning outside class.

The takeaway

Keep what works. Give practice more room.

Students rated the programme experience highly and reported skill gains. Preserve the practical, people-centred strengths while creating more supported time for technical practice, communicating findings and a manageable learning pace.
